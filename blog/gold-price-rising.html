Aaj Ka Sone Ka Bhav

Sone Ka Bhav Aaj Kyu Badh Raha Hai? 7 Bade Karan Jo Aapko Pata Hone Chahiye

Date: 12 Oct 2026 | 24K Gold Price Today: ₹1,55,290 (10 Gram) ▲ ₹420

Doston, pichle 10 dino se sone ke bhav me lagatar tezi dekhne ko mil rahi hai. Agar aap soch rahe hain ki aaj sona mehenga kyu ho gaya, to ye article aapke liye hi hai.

1. Tyohar Season - Sabse Bada Karan

India me sone ki sabse zyada demand Navratri, Dhanteras aur Diwali par hoti hai. Is samay har parivar sone ki kharidari karta hai. Demand 30-40% badhne se price automatic badh jata hai.

2. Dollar Kamzor, Gold Strong

International market ka rule hai - jab US Dollar kamzor hota hai, to Gold mehenga hota hai. Is week Dollar Index 0.8% gira hai, isliye India me bhi bhav badha hai.

3. RBI Lagatar Sona Kharid Raha Hai

Reserve Bank of India ne is saal 40 ton se zyada gold kharida hai. Jab bade banks kharidte hain to market me shortage hoti hai aur price badhta hai.

4. Mehengai (Inflation) Se Bachav

Mehengai badhne par log apne paise ko safe rakhne ke liye Gold me invest karte hain. Gold ko inflation ke against sabse safe investment mana jata hai.

5. Share Market Me Giravat

Is hafte Nifty 500 points gira hai. Jab share market girta hai to investors paisa nikal kar gold me lagate hain, isse gold ka bhav badhta hai.

6. Duniya Me Tension

America-China aur dusre desho me tension badhne par bhi log safe investment ke liye gold kharidte hain.

7. Kya Ab Sona Kharidna Chahiye?

Experts ke mutabik, agar aap 2-3 saal ke liye invest kar rahe hain to abhi kharidna sahi hai. Diwali tak 24K gold ₹1,62,000 tak ja sakta hai. Ek sath sara paisa mat lagayein, har mahine thoda-thoda kharidein (Gold SIP).

💡 Investment Tip: Physical gold ke sath-sath Digital Gold aur Gold ETF bhi dekhein, usme making charges nahi lagte.
