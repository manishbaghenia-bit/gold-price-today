Namaste dosto! Agar aap Delhi me aaj ka sone ka rate dekhne aaye ho to bilkul sahi jagah par ho. Mai Manish Kumar, Palwal se, rozana aapke liye live gold price update karta hu.

Aaj Delhi me Sone Ka Bhav (10 Gram)

  • 24K Gold (999) - ₹ 74,500 / 10 Gram - Sabse Shudh
  • 22K Gold (916) - ₹ 68,300 / 10 Gram - Jewellery ke liye best
  • 18K Gold (750) - ₹ 55,900 / 10 Gram

22K aur 24K me Kya Fark Hai?

Simple hai - 24K sona 99.9% shudh hota hai, investment ke liye best hai. 22K me 91.6% sona hota hai, isme thoda copper milaya jata hai taaki jewellery majboot bane. Shaadi ke liye 22K lena samajhdari hai.

Sona Mehenga Kyu Ho Raha Hai?

3 main kaaran hai:
1. Dollar kamzor ho raha hai
2. Shaadi ka season chal raha hai, demand high hai
3. International market me bhi gold upar ja raha hai
